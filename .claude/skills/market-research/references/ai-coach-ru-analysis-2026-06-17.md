## Key Findings

### Pricing Landscape (RU Reality)
| Model | Examples | RUB equiv | Notes |
|-------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Monthly sub | Insona, Freudly, Rocky | ₽1,300–1,800 | Auto-renew, cancel anytime |
| Annual (40-60% off) | F/AI, Zing, Rocky | ₽6,000–12,000 | Best LTV |
| One-time | CoachMe AI | ~₽300 | Low ARPU |
| Freemium | Reflectly, UTP, TG bots | — | Hard to monetize in RU without local billing |
| B2B/White-label | Rocky ($99-199/mo) | ₽9,000-18,000 | Higher ARPU, longer sales cycle |

**Critical RU Payment Insight**: F/AI built dedicated `pay.fitgpt.pro` for RU cards. Insona/Freudly use App Store/GP billing. Direct Stripe/Paddle **does not work** for RU cards.

## 3 GTM INSIGHTS (Actionable)

### 1️⃣ Telegram-First = Unfair Advantage in RU
**Why**: Dola AI, Колесо баланса, Чекеры — native TG bots with 50K-500K+ users, CAC ≈ $0. No major AI coach has native TG bot as primary interface.

**Apply**: MVP = TG bot (not App Store). Morning focus + evening reflection + 24/7 chat in one window. Payments via Telegram Stars + web link for annual. Distribution via 20-30 niche TG channels + TJ/VC collabs.

**Metric**: TG notifications open 3-4× more than App Store push.

---

### 2️⃣ "Unpacking" as Lead Magnet → Convert to Subscription
**Why**: Insona takes 40 min for "unpacking" → 10K+ paying at $14.99. Freudly gives free first session → converts to $18.99/mo.

**Apply**: Free 5-7 min "AI audit" (15-20 adaptive questions) → HTML report ("Your Growth Map: 3 priorities, 5 barriers, 30-day plan") → "Want AI coach to guide you daily? → Subscribe."

**Price Anchor**: ₽4,900/year (≈$5.4/mo) vs competitors $15-19/mo = **"3x cheaper than live coach/Insona/Freudly"**

---

### 3️⃣ Hybrid Monetization: Free(TG) → Pro(Web/App) → B2B
**Why**: CoachMe = €2.99 one-time (low ARPU). Rocky = Free tier → $9.99 Pro. Insona/Freudly = paid only (high barrier).

**Apply**:
| Tier | Includes | Price | Target Metric |
|------|----------|-------|---------------|
| **Free (TG bot)** | AM focus (1 goal/day), PM check-in, limited chat (5/day), weekly digest | ₽0 | D1 Ret ≥ 40%, D7 ≥ 20% |
| **Pro (₽490/mo / ₽4,900/yr)** | Unlimited 24/7 chat, personal plan, quarterly deep audit, progress dashboard, Notion/PDF export | ₽490 / ₽4,900 | Free→Pro ≥ 5%, ARPU ≥ ₽300 |
| **Team/B2B (₽9,900/mo for 5 seats)** | Admin panel, corporate goals, coach panel for HR/L&D, white-label | ₽9,900 | B2B pipeline, LTV ≥ 12 mo |

## Onboarding Benchmark (Best Practices)

| Stage | F/AI (23 params) | Insona (40 min) | Zing (Body Scan) | Freudly (Trial) |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Goal | Mass/cut/strength/form | 6 life spheres | Visualize target | "What bothers you" |
| Context | Experience, equipment, health, sleep, stress | Deep personality audit | Apple Health + photo + test | Dialog + clarifying Qs |
| Personalization | Plan + exercise swaps | 28 topics + AI coach | 6 pillars → program | CBT plan + tests |
| First value | **~3 min** | **40 min** (high commit) | **~5 min** (scan) | **~10 min** (trial) |

**Winner**: F/AI — optimal depth × speed balance.
