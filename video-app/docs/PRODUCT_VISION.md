# PRODUCT VISION

You are a world-class Product Manager, Growth Engineer, Viral Content Strategist, UX Designer and AI Architect.

Your task is to redesign my AI Video Generator into a Viral Content Creation Platform.

Current state:

* User enters a prompt.
* AI generates a video.

This is NOT enough.

The platform must help users create content that gets attention, retention, engagement and conversions.

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Users do not want videos.

Users want:

* views
* followers
* sales
* leads
* engagement
* authority
* emotional impact

Video generation is only a tool.

The product should transform:

IDEA → STORY → VIDEO

instead of

PROMPT → VIDEO

# REQUIRED FEATURES

Create a new workflow:

Step 1:
Select Goal

Options:

* Viral Video
* Product Promotion
* Personal Brand
* Storytelling
* Educational Content
* Meme Content
* Motivation
* Cyberpunk / Existential
* Custom

Step 2:
AI Content Strategy

AI should automatically generate:

* Hook
* Curiosity Gap
* Emotional Arc
* Tension
* Payoff
* Call To Action

Step 3:
Story Builder

AI creates a storyboard.

For each scene:

* scene goal
* narration
* visual description
* emotional target
* duration

Step 4:
Video Generation

Generate scenes individually.

Maintain style consistency.

Step 5:
Optimization

AI analyzes the generated video and predicts:

* hook strength
* retention potential
* emotional impact
* shareability
* virality score
* CTA effectiveness

# VIRALITY FRAMEWORK

Every generated video should follow:

HOOK
↓
TENSION
↓
PAYOFF
↓
ACTION

Never allow videos that start slowly.

The first 3 seconds must be optimized for stopping scroll behavior.

# DIFFERENTIATOR

Most AI video tools generate visuals.

This platform generates outcomes.

The user should feel like they have an AI Creative Director.

# SPECIAL MODE

Add a mode called:

"STATUS: WTF"

Generate philosophical, cyberpunk, existential, absurdist and psychologically powerful content.

Examples:

"Reality marked it as expected behavior."

"User lost everything."

"User still operational."

The generated content should feel like:

* cyberpunk
* philosophy
* dark humor
* internet culture
* emotional authenticity
